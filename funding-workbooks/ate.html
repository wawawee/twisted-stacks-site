6.1 Break-even math (Seed scenario)

Assumption A13. Steady-state monthly burn at ~4 FTE ≈ SEK 460k/mo (≈ SEK 5.5M/yr; payroll-dominant). Break-even = recurring gross margin ≥ monthly burn.

Path to break-evenDeals neededLogic
Enterprise-led~2–4 enterprise licenses at USD 150k/yr (~SEK 1.6M/yr each)4 × SEK 1.6M ≈ SEK 6.3M/yr > SEK 5.5M burn → break-even ~4 enterprise logos
Team-license-led~15–25 team licenses at USD 40k/yr (~SEK 420k/yr each)15 × SEK 420k ≈ SEK 6.3M/yr → break-even ~15 team logos
Cloud-seat-led~400–600 paid seats at USD 100/mo (~SEK 1,050/mo)slower/lower-ACV; realistically a complement, not the primary path pre-Series-A
Read. The fastest credible path to break-even is enterprise/team B2B licensing (2–4 enterprise or ~15 team logos), not retail cloud seats. This is also the lowest-regulatory-risk path (professional operators, not consumers). Cloud seats and module licenses layer on top.

Break-even inside 24 months is plausible only under the base/upside enterprise-led case and likely requires the Seed or Seed+ raise; Lean is a bridge, not a break-even plan.

6.3 ROI framing for funders (software multiples, not trading returns)

Guardrail. ROI here = equity/loan return via a software business (ARR × SaaS multiple), never trading P&L. Illustrative only: if ATE reaches, say, SEK 6–12M ARR at a typical infra-SaaS revenue multiple, the equity value supports a standard seed→Series-A step-up. Actual outcomes depend on execution and are not guaranteed. No performance-based return is offered or implied.

7. Red flags for grants — and how to neutralize each

Grant bodies (Vinnova, Almi, EIC) and many angels are allergic to trading/speculation optics. ATE must be pre-emptively de-risked.

#Red flagWhy it kills fundingNeutralization
R1Gambling / speculation optics ("a bot that trades for money")Public grants fund civilian innovation / dev-tools / safety, not speculation; reviewers reject P&L-seeking projectsFrame ATE as developer/infrastructure tooling + responsible-AI risk-governance. Lead with typed-swarm, HITL, observability, auditability. Trading is the domain, not the pitch. Emphasize paper-only, no capital at risk.
R2Unlicensed-advice / fund riskIf ATE looks like it advises or manages money, it needs MiFID/MiCA/FI authorization → grants won't touch itHold the "infra, not advice" line (market-research.md §3): no custody, no advice, no execution-for-clients, customer-operated. Attach a legal opinion confirming non-regulated status.
R3"AI hedge fund" framingSignals a fund (AIFMD/MiFID), performance claims, and hype-risk; reviewers/angels discount itBan the phrase. Position as trading infrastructure for teams/desks. Contrast explicitly with "no-human-intervention" agent funds (e.g., Fund Slug) — ATE is HITL-by-default.
R4No track record / fabricated performanceAny invented Sharpe/return is instantly disqualifying and reputationally fatalInvent nothing. Use TRUTH-STATUS.md verbatim posture. Sell architecture + methodology + governance, and state plainly that performance would need independent audit before any claim.
R5Entity ambiguityGrants require a clear, eligible Swedish legal applicantConfirm TwistedStacks AB registration, ownership, SME status, and de-minimis state-aid history before applying.
R6Crowded "agent trading" category2026 has many entrants (Piythia, QuantOptimus, KelAI, Nick, Fere AI…) → "why you?"Differentiate on openness (OSS core) + typed contracts + durable HITL + observability + honesty. Defensibility is the governance/coordination substrate, not another autonomous fund.

8. Recommendation

  • Raise the Seed (6 MSEK) as the primary plan: it funds Phases 1–6 and the path to first paying design-partners (M7) within 24 months, with a credible enterprise-led break-even at ~4 logos.
  • Blend instruments (see funding-plan.md): angel/pre-seed VC + Almi innovationslån as the backbone, a narrowly-scoped responsible-AI/dev-tools grant angle where defensible, accelerator for network/validation.
  • Bank the capital-efficiency story: paper-only, low data/GPU burn, no speculative capital at risk — a rare "safe" profile in a hype-heavy category.
  • Confirm TwistedStacks AB and secure the "infra-not-advice" legal opinion before any submission.
