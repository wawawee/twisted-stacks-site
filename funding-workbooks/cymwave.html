0. ⚠️ Compliance rule (read first — non-negotiable)

CymWave is a wellness / relaxation / sensory-experience / premium-hospitality product. It makes NO medical, therapeutic, diagnostic, preventive-medical, or curative claims — ever.

>

- Do not use: "treats," "cures," "therapy," "therapeutic," "detox," "heals," "medical," "clinical outcome," "diagnoses," "prevents [disease]," "improves circulation/immunity" (as medical claims), "trauma/anxiety/depression/insomnia treatment," "neurological reset," "guaranteed sleep."
- Do use: relaxation, recovery ritual, immersive spa journey, perceived stress reduction, sleep preparation, guest wellbeing, sensory experience, premium amenity, "designed for wellbeing and sensory relaxation, not diagnosis or treatment."
- Why it matters: any medical/therapeutic claim risks triggering EU MDR (2017/745) classification, which imposes clinical-evidence and conformity burdens CymWave is not resourced for, and would sink a wellness-framed grant application. It also protects grant credibility (Vinnova explicitly wants wellbeing innovation, not unsupported "healing" claims — per repo docs/GRANT_STRATEGY.md).
- Control: a claims-review checklist gates ALL outputs — grant text, pitch decks, operator scripts, brochures, website, PR. One stray word can reclassify the product. Owner sign-off required before anything ships. (See §7.)

3. Grant angles (three framings, ranked by fit)

CymWave can be framed three ways; use the framing that matches each funder (detailed instrument ranking in funding-plan.md).

Angle A — Test & Demo / testbädd (SME de-risking before market) — strongest immediate fit

  • Story: "A Swedish SME with an innovative wellness-experience solution needs to verify function, performance, safety and usability in a real test/demo environment (a partner hotel/spa + an external vibration/acoustics/water-safety test provider) before market introduction."
  • Fits: Vinnova Test & Demo för SMF 2026 (see funding-plan.md, VERIFIED). CymWave buys external test/demo services (vibration testing, water/electrical safety verification, UX testing) — exactly the eligible cost type.
  • Why it wins: frames spend as innovation-risk reduction, not product development or marketing; matches the aviation-vibration-testing track already in the repo.

Angle B — Tourism & experience innovation (besöksnäring / visitor economy)

  • Story: "Exportable Swedish experience innovation that strengthens the competitiveness and non-room revenue of Nordic hospitality — a differentiating premium amenity for hotels, spas, and retreats."
  • Fits: Tillväxtverket visitor-economy/ERUF regional priorities, Region Gävleborg företagsstöd/innovationsstöd, Visit Sweden-adjacent tourism-innovation initiatives.
  • Why it fits Gävleborg: regional development strategy + smart-specialisation (RIS3); a local wellness-tourism flagship is politically and economically attractive.

Angle C — Preventive wellbeing with a public partner (region/kommun)

  • Story: "Public-private collaboration on preventive wellbeing / stress-recovery environments (workplace wellbeing, staff recovery, healthy-workplace pilots) with a region or kommun as partner — measurable wellbeing outcomes, explicitly non-medical."
  • Fits: Vinnova collaboration/"healthy & resilient societies" calls (require a public + business actor); regional preventive-health initiatives.
  • ⚠️ Claim discipline critical here: "preventive wellbeing" must stay experiential (perceived stress, sleep readiness, comfort) and never drift into rehab/medical treatment, or it triggers MDR and disqualifies the wellness framing. A public partner is required and hardest to line up quickly.

Recommended sequence: lead with Angle A (Test & Demo) now; use Angle B for regional co-funding; pursue Angle C only once a public partner + tight non-medical protocol exist.

5. Three funding levels & break-even logic

Mapped to the repo's suggested tracks (100k / 500k / 1M / 2M) and consolidated into three actionable levels. Instrument details, deadlines, and VERIFIED/UNVERIFIED status in funding-plan.md.

Level 1 — Lean validation (~100,000–600,000 SEK)

  • Instruments: Almi Verifieringsmedel (up to 100k SEK, no co-finance) + Region Gävleborg företagsstöd/innovationsstöd (via Almi/Movexum) + own medfinansiering.
  • Covers: WP1 (validation), WP2 (FTO memo), start of WP4 (safety scoping), claims checklist.
  • Goal: de-risk the idea, produce FTO + WTP evidence + LOIs → makes the Vinnova application fundable.
  • Break-even: not expected; this is pre-revenue de-risking. Output = a credible larger application.

Level 2 — Test & Demo prototype (~1,000,000–1,400,000 SEK Vinnova + co-finance)

  • Instruments: Vinnova Test & Demo för SMF 2026 (max 1M SEK, up to 70% of eligible costs → implies ≥ ~430k SEK own/other medfinansiering to reach ~1.43M eligible) + Almi Innovationslån (50k–500k SEK) for the co-finance/prototype gap.
  • Covers: WP3 (vibration testing), WP4/WP5 (safety + proof-of-experience prototype), WP6 (journey UX).
  • Goal: a safe, tested proof-of-experience unit + verified safety/performance data → ready for a real pilot.
  • Break-even: still pre-commercial; sets up Phase 2 pilot.

Level 3 — Pilot + path to market (~2,000,000+ SEK, blended)

  • Instruments: Vinnova collaboration/"healthy & resilient societies" (up to 2M SEK, needs public + business partner — Angle C) or Tillväxtverket/ERUF regional (as a pilot company inside a larger project) + first pilot-install revenue + Almi loan.
  • Covers: WP7 (partner pilot), WP8 (evaluation/compliance), scale-up prep, EU-application groundwork (Eurostars/EIC later).
  • Break-even logic (post-grant commercial):

- Contribution per commercial install ≈ €45k × ~40% ≈ €18,000 ≈ 200,000 SEK (hardware+integration) + content ARR ~90,000 SEK/yr/unit.

- Assume a small fixed commercial cost base (2–3 people, support, insurance, ongoing certification) of ~2.0–2.5M SEK/yr.

- Break-even ≈ ~10–13 commercial installs/year (200k×~12 ≈ 2.4M SEK contribution) plus growing content ARR — i.e., roughly the top of the 3-yr SOM (10–40 installs) from market-research.md. Recurring content licences pull break-even earlier each year as the installed base compounds.

The operator-side payback (~15 months per install, market-research.md §4) is the sales argument; the CymWave-side break-even (~10–13 installs/yr) is the investor/grant argument. Both must be told, honestly, side by side.

7. Compliance operating procedure (claims-review checklist)

Gate every external artefact through this before it ships:

  1. No disease/condition named as something CymWave affects.
  2. No verbs: treat, cure, heal, diagnose, prevent (medical), remedy, therapy/therapeutic.
  3. Wellbeing claims are experiential & subjective (perceived stress, comfort, sleep readiness, relaxation) and, where possible, tied to measured user-experience outcomes — not clinical outcomes.
  4. Include the standing disclaimer: "CymWave is designed for wellbeing, recovery ritual, and sensory relaxation — not diagnosis, treatment, or cure of any medical condition."
  5. Owner/founder sign-off recorded.
  6. Applies to: grant applications, pitch decks, website, brochures, PR, operator training scripts, in-venue signage, guest onboarding.
