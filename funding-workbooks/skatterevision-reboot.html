ROI Model & Investment Dossier — TWISTEDSTACKS AB / SkatteRevision

Entity: TWISTEDSTACKS AB (Sandviken, Gävleborg) · Analyst: Fiskal · Date: 2026-07-25

Repo grounded: wawawee/SKATTEREVISION-REBOOT @ 90d594b.

POSITIONING GUARDRAIL. SkatteRevision is decision-support software, NOT tax advice (skatterådgivning) and NOT statutory audit (lagstadgad revision). Revenue in every scenario below is software / per-dossier licensing — never a percentage of recovered tax. The recovered-tax figures a byrå may achieve are the customer's value, not SkatteRevision's revenue line.

0. Entity status assumption (read first)

The repo's docs/GRANTS_INVENTORY.md (2026-06-12) states the applicant currently has "inget registrerat bolag" (no registered company yet) and treats TWISTEDSTACKS AB as the target legal carrier, with a "privatperson → enskild firma → AB" path. This brief models TWISTEDSTACKS AB as the operating entity per the mandate. Assumption A1: AB is registered (or will be) before any grant disbursement or revenue contract — consistent with Almi/Vinnova rules that the company need not exist at application but must exist before payout. All SEK figures are illustrative planning numbers, not audited projections.

1. Revenue model recap (the unit economics)

From market-research.md:

  • Primary: per-dossier licence — conservative 3,000 SEK, base 5,000–8,000 SEK.
  • Secondary/upsell: per-firm SaaS — 1,500–4,000 SEK/firm/mo.
  • Cost to serve a dossier is dominated by LLM inference + data compute. The repo routes across OpenRouter/Azure with a openrouter/free last-resort fallback and cost-tiering, so marginal cost per dossier is modest (assume ~150 SEK all-in incl. compute, QA overhead, support amortisation — Assumption A2). Gross margin per dossier at 3,000 SEK ≈ 95 %.

2. Conservative pilot ROI (N byråer × dossiers/year × price)

Deliberately conservative — small pilot cohort, low dossiers/byrå, floor price.

Pilot assumptions (Assumption A3):

  • N = 10 pilot byråer (out of the 1,054 already in SkatteRevision's data).
  • Dossiers per byrå per year = 40 (≈ a byrå running the tool on part of its client book; the repo's R3 work already produced ~20 lead profiles for a single partner).
  • Price per dossier = 3,000 SEK (floor).
  • Marginal cost per dossier = 150 SEK.

6. Bottom line

  • Engine largely built; the spend is productisation + compliance + GTM (~6.1 MSEK over 24 months).
  • Conservative 10-byrå pilot → ~1.2 MSEK gross revenue at floor pricing, ~95 % margin, ~6.7× value-to-price for the byrå.
  • Scale case (≤5 % of reachable byråer) → ~11 MSEK/yr at floor, ~2× at base pricing.
  • The dominant risk is legal-boundary, and it is controllable by design: fixed-price decision-support, never a cut of recovered tax, retire the success-fee model.
