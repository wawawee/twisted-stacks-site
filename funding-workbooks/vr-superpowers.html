6. DUAL-USE — the risk to actively avoid (expanded)

Why it matters: Civilian innovation grants (Vinnova, Energimyndigheten, EIT, Horizon Europe/EIC) screen for dual-use, defense, and surveillance applications. Under the EU financial regulation and Horizon Europe rules, projects with a primary military/surveillance purpose are ineligible, and even a strong surveillance framing can sink an otherwise civilian application. SUPARAYS contains three features that a careless pitch could frame as dual-use:

  1. RF/spectrum sensing (Flipper/SDR bridge) → could read as "signals interception / SIGINT."
  2. WiFi CSI through-wall presence → could read as "see people through walls / covert tracking."
  3. Any optics/RF that resemble ANPR/biometric capture (the repo's ALPR scaffold) → clearly surveillance-adjacent.

Mandatory guardrails (product + narrative):

  • Never use the words surveillance, interception, SIGINT, TSCM, spy, covert, ANPR/biometric, or "see people through walls" in any funding document, pitch, or demo.
  • Reframe RF strictly as Wi-Fi coverage/interference diagnostics and EMC/energy ("find the dead zone," "find the interfering source for network reliability/energy").
  • Reframe CSI/occupancy strictly as camera-free occupancy for HVAC/energy efficiency and comfort (privacy-preserving because no camera), motion-only, never identity.
  • Exclude ALPR entirely from grant scope — keep it out of the applicant's grant narrative (repo already labels it scaffold-only, Do not start without founder OK).
  • Keep the "receive-only by default" posture and EN-standard RF compliance front-and-center.
  • Lead every application with energy + accessibility + SME digitalization — the three unambiguously civilian, grant-favored themes.

Net: treated correctly, SUPARAYS is a textbook green + inclusion + digitalization SME story. Treated carelessly, the same hardware reads as surveillance. The framing discipline in this dossier is a funding requirement, not cosmetic.
