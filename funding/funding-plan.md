# TwistedStacks AB — Studio Grant Stack & Funding Plan

**Entity:** TWISTEDSTACKS AB — Sandviken, Gävleborg, Sweden
**Prepared:** 2026-07-25 · **Author:** Atlas (venture-studio / vertical-SaaS strategy)
**Framing:** Non-dilutive first. A small regional AI studio does **not** win one big "studio grant" — it **stacks per-product, per-project grants** across the portfolio (SUPARAYS, ATE, SkatteRevision, CymWave, …) and layers debt + regional co-funding underneath. Each product is its own applicant/consortium party; the studio is the shared operating layer that industrialises the *application-and-delivery motion*.

## 0. How to read this stack

A Swedish studio's non-dilutive capital comes in **four tiers**, each with a different owner, cadence, and risk:

| Tier | Instrument type | Dilution | Who applies | Cadence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Soft / early validation** | Vinnova Innovativa Startups, Region Gävleborg innovationsstöd, Movexum incubation | None (bidrag) | Each product AB | 1–2 windows/yr |
| **B. Project R&D grants** | Vinnova thematic calls (Avancerad digitalisering / Applied AI), Region konsultstöd | None (bidrag, 50% co-fund) | Product-led consortium | Themed windows |
| **C. Risk debt** | Almi Innovationslån / Tillväxtlån / Företagslån | None (loan, interest) | Studio or product AB | Rolling |
| **D. EU / scale** | Eurostars, EIC Accelerator | Grant (Eurostars) → grant+equity (EIC) | Lead product AB | Fixed cut-offs |

**Studio logic:** the same grant-writing, budgeting, and reporting muscle is a **shared platform cost** amortised across N products. Write once, template many. See `dossier.md` for the ROI math.

## 1. Ranked stack (by fit for a Gävleborg AI studio, today)

### #1 — Vinnova · Innovativa Startups 2026 — *best per-product entry point*
- **Amount:** Maximum **500 000 SEK** per company. `[VERIFIED:2026-07-25]`
- **Opens:** **12 October 2026** (Vinnova moved the opening date to 12 Oct). `[VERIFIED:2026-07-25]`
- **Eligibility:** Swedish AB, **≤10 years old, ≤10 employees, ≤10 MSEK** net turnover or balance sheet, no profit distribution. `[VERIFIED:2026-07-25]`
- **Use:** test customer need, business idea, willingness to pay (närma sig marknaden).
- **Diarienummer:** 2025-04831. `[VERIFIED:2026-07-25]`
- **Studio play:** each qualifying product AB (SUPARAYS, CymWave, SkatteRevision if <10 yrs) files its **own** application. Four products × up to 500k = up to **2.0 MSEK** non-dilutive in one window. This is the single most repeatable brick in the stack.
- **Source:** https://www.vinnova.se/e/innovativa-startups/innovativa-startups-2026
- **Caveat:** Full call text (eligible costs, funding rate, assessment) publishes when the call opens; historically covered up to 100% of a short (~9-month) project. `[UNVERIFIED]`

### #2 — Region Gävleborg · Innovationsstöd — *regional, early-stage, local anchor*
- **Amount:** Not published as a single headline figure; delivered **jointly with Almi Norra Mellansverige + Movexum** (the region's incubator). `[VERIFIED:2026-07-25]`
- **Eligibility:** small newly-started company, **≤5 years** from registration, product/process/service **not yet on market** — support must precede market stage. `[VERIFIED:2026-07-25]`
- **Related regional instruments (same page):**
  - **Mikrostöd:** costs max **60 000 SEK**. `[VERIFIED:2026-07-25]`
  - **Medelstora hårda investeringar:** stöd max **3 MSEK** (de minimis, over 3-year period). `[VERIFIED:2026-07-25]`
  - **Stora investeringar:** costs max **25 MSEK**; above 25 MSEK → go to Tillväxtverket; **companies in Gävle kommun cannot apply** for this type. `[VERIFIED:2026-07-25]`
  - **Konsultstöd (cirkulär omställning):** consultant costs max **70 000 SEK**, work ≤6 months, ~4-week handläggning. `[VERIFIED:2026-07-25]`
- **Studio play:** TwistedStacks is in **Sandviken** (not Gävle kommun) → the "stora investeringar" exclusion does **not** bind it, a genuine location advantage. Route new products through Movexum → Region innovationsstöd for the earliest non-dilutive kronor + local credibility.
- **Source:** https://www.regiongavleborg.se/regional-utveckling/finansiering/foretagsstod/

### #3 — Almi · Innovationslån — *bridge capital, no equity*
- **Amount:** **50 000–500 000 SEK** (in many cases ~**300 000 SEK**). `[VERIFIED:2026-07-25]`
- **Co-financing:** Almi funds up to **50%** of estimated project cost; for loans **>300 000 SEK** at least half of the co-financing must be capital (equity/bank/external). `[VERIFIED:2026-07-25]`
- **Eligibility:** company registered in Sweden, **≤249 employees**; new or established. Repayment **3–5 years**. `[VERIFIED:2026-07-25]`
- **Adjacent Almi products:** **Tillväxtlån** (for scalable companies) and **Företagslån** (no fixed upper limit; Almi typically funds **30–50%** of capital need; repayment **2–5 yrs**; rates ~2–3 pp above banks). `[VERIFIED:2026-07-25]`
- **Studio play:** use Innovationslån to bridge the gap **between** grant tranches (grants pay in arrears). Debt keeps the cap table clean — central to the non-dilutive thesis.
- **Source:** https://www.almi.se/lan-finansiering/innovationslan/

### #4 — Vinnova · Tillämpad AI för industrin (Avancerad digitalisering) — *high-fit for SUPARAYS / ATE, but consortium-gated*
- **Amount:** **2–10 MSEK per project**, grant ≤ **50%** of eligible costs; project **12–24 months**; total call budget **60 MSEK**. `[VERIFIED:2026-07-25]`
- **Two tracks:** **Intelligent Edge — AI in the sensor chain** (near-perfect fit for SUPARAYS AR sensor mesh; the call text explicitly frames edge AI + multi-agent decentralised intelligence) and **AI-based decision support** (fits ATE agentic trading infra). `[VERIFIED:2026-07-25]`
- **Eligibility:** **consortium of ≥2 parties, ≥1 Swedish company**; needs-driven; behovsägare engaged. `[VERIFIED:2026-07-25]`
- **Status:** the 2026 window **closed 16 January 2026, 14:00**. `[VERIFIED:2026-07-25]` → Track the next window under the Avancerad digitalisering programme (recurring). `[UNVERIFIED]`
- **Studio play:** this is where the studio structure pays off — TwistedStacks can be the **industrial company party** and bring a customer/behovsägare (e.g. a Sandviken/Gävleborg manufacturer or clinic) as second party. Requires the 50% co-fund, so pair with Almi debt or Region stöd.
- **Source:** https://www.vinnova.se/e/avancerad-digitalisering-industriell-2024-04161/tillampad-ai-for-industrin-genom-2025-03809/

### #5 — Tillväxtverket · "Utveckla företags konkurrenskraft i Norra Mellansverige" (ERUF) — *regional SME programme, Gävleborg-eligible*
- **Amount:** project may receive **≤60% EU co-funding**; call budget **33 MSEK** (may change). Remaining 40% = public and/or private funds. `[VERIFIED:2026-07-25]`
- **Geography:** **Gävleborg, Dalarna, Värmland**. `[VERIFIED:2026-07-25]`
- **Window:** **opens 20 August 2026, closes 22 September 2026**; decision Dec 2026 / Jan 2027. `[VERIFIED:2026-07-25]`
- **Fit:** funds förstudier/projects strengthening SME competitiveness + green transition, entrepreneurship in future industries, and the företagsfrämjande system — better suited to a **collaborative studio-ecosystem project** (e.g. with Movexum) than to a single product. `[UNVERIFIED]` fit-judgment.
- **Source:** https://tillvaxtverket.se/tillvaxtverket/sokfinansiering/utlysningar/utlysningar/utvecklaforetagskonkurrenskraftinorramellansverige.12025.html

### #6 — Tillväxtverket · Regionalt investeringsstöd — *hardware capex (SUPARAYS / CymWave)*

- **Window:** rolling via *Min ansökan*; call **closes 31 December 2026**; **>25 MSEK → Tillväxtverket** (else the region). `[VERIFIED:2026-07-25]`

### #7 — Eurostars (Eureka) · Call 11 — *international R&D, SME-led (later-stage optionality)*

### #8 — EIC Accelerator — *deep-tech scale-up; grant + equity (last, not first)*

- **2026 short-proposal cut-offs (batched, 17:00 Brussels):** **7 Jan · 4 Mar · 6 May · 8 Jul · 2 Sep · 4 Nov 2026**. `[VERIFIED:2026-07-25]`
- **Budget:** EIC Accelerator 2026 total **€634M**. `[VERIFIED:2026-07-25]`
- **Studio play:** **dilutive** and highly competitive — reserve for the single break-out product with TRL ≥6 and market-creating claim. Flagged here for completeness of the stack, not as a near-term move.
- **Source:** https://eic.ec.europa.eu/eic-funding-opportunities/eic-2026-work-programme_en · https://eic.ec.europa.eu/eic-funding-opportunities/eic-accelerator_en

## 4. Honest constraints

1. **Grants pay in arrears** and demand co-financing (Vinnova Applied-AI 50%, ERUF 40% own funds). Cash-flow, not headline award, is the binding constraint → Almi debt is structurally necessary.
2. **Consortium-gated calls (#4, #5, #7)** need a *second party* and often a *behovsägare*. A solo studio must actively recruit customers/partners — this is real work, not a formality.
3. **Regional geography is DeSO-specific.** Do not assume the 40% Regionalt investeringsstöd rate applies to Sandviken without the SCB DeSO check (flagged UNVERIFIED above).
4. **No "studio-level" mega-grant exists.** The model is *N small per-product bricks*, which multiplies **application + reporting overhead** — the very focus risk detailed in `dossier.md`.
