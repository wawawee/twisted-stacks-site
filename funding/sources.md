CymWave was **not** found in the repo — treated as an early/unlaunched portfolio bet. `[UNVERIFIED]`
2. **Entity age & size:** TWISTEDSTACKS AB and each product AB are assumed to satisfy Vinnova Innovativa Startups limits (≤10 yr, ≤10 emp, ≤10 MSEK). Not verified against Bolagsverket/allabolag; each AB's registration date must be checked before filing. `[UNVERIFIED]`
3. **Sandviken (not Gävle kommun):** assumed to place TwistedStacks outside the Region Gävleborg "stora investeringar" exclusion that binds Gävle-kommun companies. Confirmed the *exclusion wording*; not the entity's registered kommun. `[UNVERIFIED]`
4. **Regionalt investeringsstöd 40% rate:** eligibility is stödområde/DeSO-specific; Sandviken's DeSO not verified — do not assume the 40% rate applies. `[UNVERIFIED]`
5. **Illustrative studio economics** (24-month cost model, per-product grant capture, three funding levels) are **modelled scenarios**, not commitments or forecasts. Success rates for studio-built companies (survival, IRR) are drawn from cited third-party analyses (US/EU-weighted) and may not transfer to a solo regional studio. `[UNVERIFIED — modelled]`
6. **Grant "capture" assumes non-trivial win rates.** Vinnova/EU calls are competitive; a 100% capture across N products is optimistic. Scenarios apply haircut rates (see `dossier.md`). `[UNVERIFIED — modelled]`
7. **Vinnova Innovativa Startups funding rate/duration** (historically up to 100% of a ~9-month project) is based on the 2025 call; the 2026 full call text was not yet published on 2026-07-25. `[UNVERIFIED]`
8. **Eurostars Call 11 deadline** taken as 10 Sep 2026 (official call page); a secondary Eureka resources page cited 21 Jul 2026 — treated as stale. `[UNVERIFIED — conflict noted]`
9. **Comparator equity/return figures** (Hexa ~30%, studio IRR ~53%, Antler €500k) are as reported by the companies/analysts cited; not independently audited. `[UNVERIFIED]`
